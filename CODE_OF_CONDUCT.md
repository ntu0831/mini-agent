# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in MiniAgent to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone else's private information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour
they consider inappropriate. Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and
should be reported privately to the maintainers.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
